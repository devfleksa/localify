# United States (US)
Copy language: American English · Script: Latin · Currency: $1,299.99 (USD, tax added at checkout) · Date: 10/14/2026 (MM/DD/YYYY) · Numbers: 1,299.99

## Protect
- A price in another currency (₺, €, ¥, ₹, R$, SAR) or a date in DD/MM or DD.MM format.
- The US flag worn, printed on disposable items, used on the ground or altered; national symbols used as a joke.
- Metric-only measures in prices or offers (per kg, km, °C) where Americans expect pounds, miles, °F.
- Culturally loaded imagery out of context: religious symbols as decoration, Native American headdresses as costume, blackface or ethnic caricatures (review if people are involved).
- Firearms or weapons as casual props in a lifestyle scene.

## Language & formats
- American English, short and punchy, active voice; title case is fine for headlines. American spelling (color, favorite, center).
- Price: "$1,299.99" or "$49" (comma for thousands, dot for decimals). Prices usually end in .99 or .95. Sales: "30% off", "BOGO".
- Date: 10/14/2026, "Oct 14", "Thru 10/31". Time: 12-hour with AM/PM. Units: miles, feet, pounds, ounces, °F, fl oz.
- CTAs: "Shop now", "Order today", "Get yours", "Free shipping", "Limited time", "In stores now".
- Holidays that make good themes: Thanksgiving (4th Thursday of November), July 4, Black Friday, Halloween, Super Bowl Sunday, Back to School, Memorial Day and Labor Day sales.

## Objects & food
Swap an object for the American equivalent that plays the same role (same size, position and use). Describe it visually:
- **Tea glass, small coffee cup, espresso → large takeaway coffee**: tall paper cup with a plastic lid and cardboard sleeve, or a big ceramic diner mug.
- **Local grill (mangal, braai, churrasco) → backyard barbecue**: round black kettle grill or a stainless gas grill with burgers, hot dogs and corn on the cob, a red-and-white checkered tablecloth.
- **Local bread or pastry (simit, croissant, pão de queijo) → bagel or donut**: sesame or everything bagel with cream cheese; glazed ring donuts in a pink box.
- **Local street food (döner, kebab, samosa) → hot dog, burger or slice of pizza**: hot dog with mustard in a paper tray; a big New York slice folded on a paper plate.
- **Local breakfast → American diner breakfast**: stack of pancakes with butter and maple syrup, bacon, sunny-side-up eggs, orange juice.
- **Local soft drink (ayran, chai, guaraná) → iced drink**: tall clear plastic cup with ice and a straw, or a glass bottle of lemonade.
- **Holiday decor**: fall → pumpkins, hay bales, orange and red leaves; summer → small US flags and red-white-blue bunting; winter → string lights, wreaths.
- **Picnic / camping**: red cooler box, folding camp chairs, s'mores over a campfire, a pickup truck nearby.

## Places
### Billboard / skyline / big outdoor ad
- **Times Square, New York**: canyon of huge glowing LED billboards on every building, yellow cabs, crowds, the red TKTS steps.
- **Sunset Boulevard (Sunset Strip), Los Angeles**: giant hand-painted and LED billboards over a palm-lined boulevard, low buildings, Hollywood Hills behind.
- **Chicago Loop / Michigan Avenue**: steel and glass towers, the river with bascule bridges, Willis Tower and Trump Tower in view, the elevated "L" train.
- **Las Vegas Strip**: casino resorts, the Bellagio fountains, neon signs, wide boulevard with palm trees.
- **Highway billboard (I-10 / I-95 style)**: large steel-legged billboard beside a multi-lane interstate, green highway signs with white text, pickup trucks.

### Shopping street / shop window / wall poster
- **SoHo, New York**: cast-iron building façades with fire escapes, cobblestone patches, flagship stores, brick walls with wheat-paste posters.
- **Fifth Avenue, New York**: flagship stores, Art Deco towers, St. Patrick's area in the distance, wide sidewalks with yellow cabs.
- **Rodeo Drive, Beverly Hills**: palm trees, luxury boutiques with white stone façades, perfect sidewalks, sunshine.
- **Melrose Avenue, Los Angeles**: colourful painted walls and murals, low shops, street-style crowd.
- **Miami Design District / Ocean Drive**: pastel Art Deco buildings, neon signs, palm trees.

### Bus stop / street-level transit
- **New York MTA bus stop**: glass shelter with an ad panel, blue-and-white MTA bus, yellow cabs, a hot dog cart, brownstones or avenue towers.
- **Los Angeles Metro bus stop on Wilshire Boulevard**: shelter with ad panel, orange or silver Metro bus, palm trees, wide sunny boulevard.
- **San Francisco Muni stop**: steep street, cable car or historic F-line streetcar, Victorian houses.

### Metro / station
US subway stations are gritty and functional. Describe them as they really are: white or beige ceramic wall tiles with
mosaic station names, steel I-beam columns painted green or black, worn concrete platforms, fluorescent lights,
backlit ad frames, ordinary commuters. Avoid "sleek" or "futuristic".
- **New York subway (e.g. 14 St–Union Square, Times Sq–42 St)**: round coloured line bullets (red 1/2/3, yellow N/Q/R), black-and-white station signs, MetroCard/OMNY turnstiles.
- **Grand Central Terminal main concourse**: vaulted green ceiling with constellations, the golden four-faced clock, marble floors.
- **Chicago "L" elevated platform**: steel structure above the street, wooden platform, trains with coloured line signs.
- **Washington DC Metro**: deep coffered concrete vault ceilings, dim warm light, brown hexagonal floor tiles.

### Roadside / landscape / coast
- **Route 66 (Arizona)**: two-lane desert road, Route 66 shield painted on the asphalt, old diner and neon motel signs.
- **Pacific Coast Highway (Big Sur)**: winding road on cliffs above the Pacific, Bixby Creek Bridge (concrete arch).
- **Monument Valley (Utah/Arizona)**: red sandstone buttes rising from flat desert, straight road to the horizon.
- **Grand Canyon**: layered red and orange canyon walls, deep gorge, viewpoint railing.
- **Yosemite Valley**: granite cliffs (El Capitan, Half Dome), pine forest, waterfalls.
- **Miami Beach**: wide white sand, turquoise water, colourful lifeguard towers.
- **Brooklyn Bridge / Manhattan skyline from DUMBO**: stone bridge towers with cables, cobblestone street, skyline behind.
- **Rural Midwest farm road**: red barn, white fence, cornfields, a pickup truck, big sky.

### Night
Times Square fully lit, the Las Vegas Strip neon, a neon-lit roadside diner, the Golden Gate Bridge with fog and city lights.

## Keep it real
Wide roads, big cars and pickup trucks, yellow cabs in New York, fire hydrants, green street-name signs on corner poles,
traffic lights hanging over the road, US flags on porches and buildings (small), red "STOP" signs. Normal daylight,
everyday crowds of many ethnicities. Any readable sign in American English, correctly spelled.

## Never
- Never use the flag as clothing, a tablecloth, a napkin or on the ground.
- Never add guns or weapons as props.
- Never turn a scene into a political or protest scene; no politicians or campaign signs.
- Never use costumes or symbols of another culture as decoration (headdresses, sombreros as a joke).
