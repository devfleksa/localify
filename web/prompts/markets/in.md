# India (IN)
Copy language: Hindi · Script: Devanagari · Currency: ₹1,299 or ₹1,29,999 (lakh grouping) · Date: 14/10/2026 (DD/MM/YYYY) · Numbers: 1,29,999 (lakh, crore)

## Protect
- Beef shown as food (burgers, steaks): cows are sacred for most Hindus. Pork can also offend Muslim audiences.
- Gods, deities, Om or swastika-like religious symbols as decoration, on shoes, floors, clothing or products.
- Feet or shoes pointed at people, food, books or religious items; shoes in a temple or home shrine scene.
- Eating with the left hand, or passing food with the left hand.
- Alcohol as part of the ad: direct alcohol advertising is banned in India; alcohol tied to religious festivals (Diwali, Holi) is offensive.
- The Indian flag, national emblem or map used wrongly (an incorrect India map is a legal issue).
- A price in another currency, or prices without the ₹ sign.

## Language & formats
- **All localized copy in Hindi, written in Devanagari** (not English, not Hinglish in Latin script). Simple, everyday
  Hindi; common English loanwords are fine written in Devanagari (कॉफ़ी, ऑर्डर, स्टाइलिश, सेल). Warm, friendly tone.
- Rendering: tested 2026-10-15, the edit model writes two caption-size lines in this script correctly; keep new text about as long as the original, in a clean bold sans-serif.
- Price: "₹1,299" (Western digits, ₹ sign); large numbers with lakh grouping ("₹1,29,999"). Prices often end in 99 or 999. "MRP" and "incl. of all taxes" are common.
- Date: 14/10/2026 or "14 अक्टूबर". Time: 12-hour. Units: metric (km, kg, °C).
- CTAs: "अभी खरीदें" (buy now), "ऑर्डर करें" (order), "और जानें" (learn more), "ऑफ़र देखें" (see the offer), "फ्री डिलीवरी" (free delivery), "सीमित समय" (limited time), "नया" (new).
- Themes that resonate: Diwali (lamps, rangoli, sweets; biggest sale season), Holi (colours), Navratri and Durga Puja, Eid, Independence Day (15 August), Republic Day (26 January), cricket (IPL).

## Objects & food
Swap for the Indian equivalent that plays the same role (same size, position and use). Describe it visually:
- **Beef burger, steak → vegetarian or chicken dish**: aloo tikki burger, paneer tikka on skewers, tandoori chicken; or a veg thali.
- **Takeaway coffee, mug → masala chai**: small clay kulhad cup or a short glass of milky orange-brown chai; a steel tumbler of South Indian filter coffee on a steel dabarah.
- **Barbecue, grill → tandoor or seekh kebab grill**: clay tandoor with naan on the wall, or a charcoal grill with seekh kebabs and paneer tikka on skewers, onion rings and lemon.
- **Sandwich, wrap, hot dog → Indian street food**: vada pav (spiced potato fritter in a bun), samosas with green chutney, kathi roll in paper.
- **Pizza → pav bhaji or dosa**: buttery pav buns next to a bowl of red bhaji; a long crisp golden dosa with sambar and coconut chutney.
- **Cake, cookies, dessert → Indian sweets (mithai)**: gulab jamun in syrup, laddoos, kaju katli with silver leaf in a decorated box.
- **Breakfast → Indian breakfast**: idli with sambar and chutneys, or aloo paratha with butter, curd and pickle.
- **Soft drink → lassi or nimbu pani**: tall glass of thick lassi with a layer of cream, or lemonade with mint.
- **Holiday decor, Christmas lights → Diwali decor** (only if timing fits): rows of clay diyas (oil lamps), a colourful rangoli pattern on the floor, marigold garlands, string lights.
- **Lucky charm → nimbu-mirchi** (lemon and green chillies on a thread) or a marigold garland.
- **Picnic → picnic with steel tiffin boxes**: stacked stainless-steel tiffin carrier, a durrie (cotton rug).

## Places
### Billboard / skyline / big outdoor ad
- **Bandra–Worli Sea Link, Mumbai**: cable-stayed bridge with a tall concrete pylon over the sea, Mumbai skyline behind.
- **Western Express Highway, Mumbai**: wide highway lined with dense hoardings, auto-rickshaws, flyovers.
- **Cyber Hub, Gurugram**: modern glass office towers, wide plazas, bright signage.
- **Connaught Place, Delhi**: white Georgian colonnaded circle of buildings, big hoardings on rooftops, a large Indian flag at Central Park.
- **Lower Parel / BKC, Mumbai**: new glass towers next to old mills, busy roads with black-and-yellow taxis.

### Shopping street / shop window / wall poster
- **Linking Road / Hill Road, Bandra (Mumbai)**: busy shopping street, street stalls, colourful shop signs, crowds, auto-rickshaws.
- **Khan Market, Delhi**: compact upscale market, two-storey buildings, boutiques, cafés.
- **Commercial Street / UB City, Bengaluru**: shopping street with fashion stores; UB City's luxury mall with modern towers.
- **Johari Bazaar, Jaipur**: uniform pink-painted façades with arched shopfronts, jewellery shops.
- **Park Street, Kolkata**: colonial buildings, old restaurants and bakeries, yellow Ambassador taxis.

### Bus stop / street-level transit
- **BEST bus stop, Mumbai**: shelter with an ad panel, red BEST buses (some double-decker), black-and-yellow Premier Padmini taxis, auto-rickshaws.
- **Delhi bus stop**: shelter with ad panel, green or red DTC low-floor buses, green-and-yellow CNG auto-rickshaws.
- **Auto-rickshaw stand**: rows of three-wheel auto-rickshaws (yellow-and-green in Delhi, black-and-yellow in Mumbai).

### Metro / station
Indian metro stations are new, clean and busy: grey granite floors, stainless-steel railings, bright lights, bilingual
signs (Hindi and English, plus the regional language), security checkpoints, crowded rush hours. Not futuristic.
- **Delhi Metro (Rajiv Chowk)**: line colours (yellow, blue) on signs, platform screen doors, crowds.
- **Mumbai Metro Line 1 / Line 3**: elevated or underground platforms, blue signage.
- **Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Terminus (CST), Mumbai**: Victorian Gothic station with domes, turrets and arches; packed local trains (exterior or concourse).
- **Mumbai local train**: crowded blue-and-yellow or maroon local trains with open doors, platform with commuters.

### Roadside / landscape / coast
- **Marine Drive, Mumbai**: curved seafront promenade with Art Deco buildings, palm trees, sea wall with tetrapods; "Queen's Necklace" at night.
- **Kerala backwaters (Alleppey)**: wooden houseboats with thatched roofs on calm green water, coconut palms.
- **Ladakh (Pangong Lake / Leh roads)**: barren brown mountains, deep blue lake, prayer flags, winding mountain roads with motorcycles.
- **Goa beaches**: palm-lined beach, colourful shacks, fishing boats on the sand.
- **Rajasthan desert (Jaisalmer)**: golden sand dunes, sandstone fort, camels.
- **Western Ghats / Munnar**: rolling green tea plantations on hills, mist.
- **Gateway of India, Mumbai**: basalt arch monument on the waterfront, boats, the Taj Mahal Palace hotel beside it.
- **Indian highway (NH48)**: wide highway with trucks painted in bright colours ("Horn OK Please"), dhabas by the road.

### Night
Marine Drive's lights at night, Connaught Place lit up, Diwali-lit streets with diyas and string lights, busy night markets with street-food stalls.

## Keep it real
Busy, colourful, layered streets: auto-rickshaws, two-wheelers, painted trucks, street vendors, hand-painted shop signs,
overhead wires, warm dusty light, many people. Not empty or overly polished. Readable signs in English or correct
Hindi; keep them few and short.

## Never
- Never add beef or pork dishes, or show cows in a negative or comic way.
- Never put deities, temples or religious symbols behind or on a commercial product.
- Never show shoes on tables, near food or in religious spaces.
- Never use an incorrect map of India.
