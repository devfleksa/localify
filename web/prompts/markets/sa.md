# Saudi Arabia (SA)
Copy language: Arabic · Script: Arabic, right-to-left · Currency: 1,299 ر.س or SAR 1,299 · Date: 14/10/2026 (Gregorian; Hijri for religious dates) · Numbers: Western 1,299 or Arabic-Indic ١٬٢٩٩

## Protect
- Alcohol in any form: beer, wine, cocktails, champagne glasses, bars (illegal in Saudi Arabia).
- Pork or pork products: bacon, ham, salami, pork sausages, hot dogs that look like pork.
- Religious symbols of other faiths as decoration (crosses, Christmas nativity, Buddha statues), and Islamic symbols or Quranic text used commercially or on products.
- Dogs as indoor or family-lifestyle props (seen as unclean by many).
- Eating, drinking or snacking shown in public during a Ramadan campaign.
- Gestures: showing the sole of the foot or shoe toward people, eating or offering food with the left hand.
- Romantic or intimate couple scenes, and exposed bodies (shoulders, legs, cleavage): review only, never edit.
- A price in another currency or a USD/EUR-only offer.

## Language & formats
- **All localized copy in Arabic** (Modern Standard with Gulf warmth). Respectful, family-oriented tone. If the original
  is already bilingual (Arabic + English), keep that layout.
- Arabic is right-to-left: replace English text with Arabic in the same box, right-aligned. Keep the brand name in its original script.
- Rendering: tested 2026-10-15, the edit model writes two caption-size lines in this script correctly; keep new text about as long as the original, in a clean bold sans-serif. Letters must be joined, right-to-left.
- Price: "١٬٢٩٩ ر.س" or "SAR 1,299" or "1,299 ريال". Western digits are common in retail.
- Date: 14/10/2026; religious occasions by the Hijri calendar. The weekend is Friday–Saturday.
- CTAs: "تسوّق الآن" (shop now), "اطلب الآن" (order now), "اكتشف المزيد" (discover more), "عرض لفترة محدودة" (limited-time offer), "جديد" (new).
- Themes that resonate: Ramadan (crescent, lanterns, iftar), Eid al-Fitr and Eid al-Adha, Saudi National Day (23 September, green), Founding Day (22 February), Riyadh Season.

## Objects & food
Swap for the Saudi equivalent that plays the same role (same size, position and use). Describe it visually:
- **Wine, beer, champagne, cocktail → non-alcoholic drink**: Saudi champagne (sparkling apple juice with mint and sliced fruit in a glass jug), fresh juice, or sparkling water in the same glass shape.
- **Coffee cup, mug, takeaway coffee → Arabic coffee (gahwa)**: brass or gold dallah coffee pot with a long curved spout, small handleless finjan cups with pale gold coffee, a plate of dates.
- **Tea glass → Arabic tea**: small clear glass of red tea with mint on a gold-rimmed tray.
- **Bacon, ham, pork sausage → halal alternative**: beef bacon strips, turkey slices, or grilled beef sausages.
- **Dessert, cookies, cake → dates and Arabic sweets**: Ajwa or Sukkari dates in a carved wooden or gold tray; kunafa with orange cheese and pistachio; maamoul cookies.
- **Burger, sandwich → shawarma or mutabbaq**: chicken shawarma wrap in paper; folded mutabbaq pastry.
- **Barbecue, picnic grill → Saudi kabsa or mandi tray / desert campfire**: large round tray of spiced rice with roasted chicken or lamb; a campfire in the sand with a dallah on the coals.
- **Camping, picnic → desert camp (kashta)**: red-and-black patterned floor cushions and carpets on the sand, a low tent, lanterns, dallah and dates.
- **Christmas tree, Christmas decor → Ramadan or Eid decor** (only if the campaign timing fits): fanous lanterns, crescent moon lights, gold stars; otherwise neutral festive lights.
- **Dog as a pet prop → falcon or Arabian horse** (only for outdoor heritage scenes), or simply remove the dog's scene role with a neutral object.
- **Lucky charm, evil eye → incense burner (mabkhara)** with oud smoke.

## Places
### Billboard / skyline / big outdoor ad
- **Kingdom Centre Tower, Riyadh**: tall tower with a huge upside-down parabolic opening and a sky bridge at the top, King Fahd Road below.
- **Al Faisaliah Tower, Riyadh**: pointed glass tower with a golden sphere near the top.
- **King Abdullah Financial District (KAFD), Riyadh**: cluster of modern glass towers with sharp shapes, wide clean plazas, the KAFD monorail.
- **King Fahd Road, Riyadh**: multi-lane highway lined with tall towers and large digital billboards.
- **Jeddah Corniche**: seafront road with the King Fahd's Fountain jet in the Red Sea, palm trees, modern towers.

### Shopping street / shop window / wall poster
- **Tahlia Street (Prince Mohammed bin Abdulaziz Road), Riyadh**: upscale boulevard with flagship stores, cafés, palm trees, evening lights.
- **Riyadh Park or Kingdom Centre mall**: large bright mall atrium, marble floors, international store fronts.
- **Via Riyadh**: luxury open-air shopping area with beige stone buildings in Najdi style and arcades.
- **Al-Balad (historic Jeddah)**: coral-stone houses with dark wooden mashrabiya balconies, narrow lanes, souk stalls.
- **Diriyah (At-Turaif / Bujairi Terrace)**: mud-brick Najdi buildings with triangular openings, palm trees, elegant terraces.

### Bus stop / street-level transit
- **Riyadh bus stop (RCRC)**: modern air-conditioned glass shelter with an ad panel, Riyadh city buses, wide road with palm trees.
- **Riyadh street corner**: white Toyota Land Cruisers and SUVs, taxis, wide sidewalks, bilingual Arabic-English street signs.

### Metro / station
- **Riyadh Metro, KAFD station**: striking modern station with wavy light-coloured façade and patterned openings (designed by Zaha Hadid Architects). This is a real, genuinely modern network (opened 2024–2025): clean but realistic.
- **Riyadh Metro platform**: platform screen doors, line colours (blue, red, orange, yellow, green, purple), bilingual signs.
- **Haramain High-Speed Railway station (Jeddah)**: large hall with tall white columns branching like palm trees.

### Roadside / landscape / coast
- **Edge of the World (Jebel Fihrayn), near Riyadh**: sheer limestone cliffs over a vast flat plain, dusty track to the edge.
- **AlUla**: towering sandstone rock formations, Elephant Rock, Hegra's Nabataean tombs carved into rock, Maraya mirrored building in the desert.
- **Empty Quarter / red sand dunes**: tall orange-red dunes, 4x4 tracks, a desert camp at sunset.
- **Asir (Abha) mountains**: green terraced mountains, fog, colourful painted Asiri houses.
- **Red Sea coast**: turquoise water, coral, white sand (Umluj, Red Sea Project).
- **Desert highway**: straight road through dunes with camel crossing signs and camels by the road.

### Night
Riyadh skyline at night with Kingdom Centre lit, Boulevard City's lights and screens (Riyadh Season), Jeddah Corniche promenade lights, Ramadan lantern-lit streets.

## Keep it real
Wide highways, big white SUVs and pickups, palm trees, beige and sand-coloured buildings, bilingual Arabic-English signs
(green road signs with white text), strong bright sunlight. People in the distance in everyday Saudi dress (men in white
thobes, women in black abayas) only if the original scene has people in the distance; never edit the main people.
Any readable sign in correct Arabic, right-to-left, or in English.

## Never
- Never add alcohol, pork, bars, nightclubs or casinos.
- Never put mosques, the Kaaba, or Quranic text behind or into an ad.
- Never add crosses, other religious symbols, or zodiac/occult decor.
- Never add dogs to a home or lifestyle scene.
- Never edit people's dress to make them "more modest": that is a review card for a human.
