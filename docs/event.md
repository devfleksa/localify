- **Name:** 10X Your Brand Hackathon, Istanbul Ignited by Slush'D

- **Place:** DotsHub, Beşiktaş, Istanbul
- **Hosts:** Istanbul Ignited by Slush'D (Kaan Taskent, Ipek Celiksoz)

- **Audience:** founders, marketers, designers, creatives; beginners welcome

## Challenge
Build a campaign, landing page, brand system, content engine, automated workflow,
or launch-ready concept. Possible starting points:
- an existing brand to upgrade
- a new idea to launch
- **a repetitive marketing workflow to automate** (our track)

## Judging criteria (no weights given)
1. **Transformation:** how meaningful and visible is the before-and-after?
2. **Speed:** how much was genuinely built and shipped in the time available?
3. **Use of Manus:** how meaningfully did Manus orchestrate work across tools, context, workflows?
4. **Real-world utility:** could it go live tomorrow and generate measurable value?
